# Meeting Preparation

Generate preparation notes for an upcoming meeting including:
- Relevant tasks and their current status
- Key discussion points based on recent activity
- Open questions or decisions needed
- Action items from previous related meetings

## Context
- Focus on tasks assigned to meeting participants
- Include recent updates from the past week
- Reference any linked documents or notes

## Output Format
Structure as a meeting prep document with:
1. **Meeting Overview**: Quick summary of purpose
2. **Status Updates**: Current state of relevant work
3. **Discussion Topics**: Key points to cover
4. **Open Questions**: Items needing decisions
5. **Suggested Actions**: Next steps to propose

## Tone
- Clear and actionable
- Focused on enabling productive discussion
